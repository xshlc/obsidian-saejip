# saejip, an Obsidian starter vault

> 새집, a homonym, means "nest" or "new house"

> **saejip** _(새집)_  
> **noun** · Korean

### Definition

1. **A new house or home**; a newly built or newly acquired residence.
2. **A bird’s nest**, depending on context and pronunciation/meaning.

### Usage

The meaning of **saejip (새집)** depends on the context in which it is used. When referring to a person’s residence, it commonly means **“new house”** or **“new home.”** In other contexts, **새집** can refer to a **bird’s nest**.

### Etymology

From Korean **새** (“new”) + **집** (“house, home”), giving the literal meaning **“new house.”** The same written form can also represent **새집**, meaning “bird’s nest.”

### Example

> _We finally moved into our saejip (새집) last week._

**English equivalents:** _new house, new home; bird’s nest_
